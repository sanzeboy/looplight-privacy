# Looplight – Privacy Policy

*Effective 25 November 2026*

Looplight is a single-player arcade game for Android made by Sanjeeb Boyaju ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to **sanjeebboyaju@gmail.com**.

## What the game stores about you

**Your game progress.** Your coins and gems, your heroes, gear and talents, your best stages, your quests, daily rewards and calendar, the items you have unlocked with in-game coins and gems, and your settings (sound and vibration). This is saved in a file on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is also backed up to your Google Play Games saved games (see below), so that your progress can come back on a new phone. It is never sent to us or to a server of ours, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time in **Settings › Delete save data**.

**No account with us.** Looplight has no account of its own. We do not ask for your name, your email address or any other contact details. If you use Google Play Games, the game sees only your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), as described below.

We do not collect your contacts, your photos, your files, your microphone or camera, or your precise location. The game has no chat and no online multiplayer.

## Google Play Games

The game uses Google Play Games Services for leaderboards (Furthest Stage, Endless Mode and Daily Challenge) and to back up your progress. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and your scores are processed by Google to show them to you and on the leaderboards, and your progress is backed up to your Google Play Games saved games, so it can be restored when you install the game on another device. This is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we do not receive your Google account details. You can play without signing in; the game then keeps your progress on your device only. **Settings** shows whether you are signed in.

## Advertising

Looplight is free and is supported only by ads served by Google AdMob. Nothing in the game is sold for real money: there are no in-app purchases. There are occasional full-screen ads between runs (never during play), and optional video ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game reward, such as a revive, double coins, a reroll, a Flare start or free gems in the shop. There are no banner ads.

To show ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), information about your device, and information about how you interact with the ads, as well as crash and performance information about the ads SDK. Google uses this to show ads, to measure them, and to prevent fraud and abuse. This information goes to Google, not to us.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before AdMob uses your information for personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in **Settings › Privacy options**. Wherever you are, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Service providers

- **Google:** AdMob advertising, Google Play Games leaderboards and saved games, and Google Play for distributing the app. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Unity Technologies:** the game is built with the Unity engine. The engine itself may collect basic, non-identifying crash and device information. See the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

We do not sell your information. We do not receive or store any personal information ourselves.

## How long information is kept, and deleting it

Your game progress stays on your device until you delete it in Settings, clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Each of these deletes it from the device for good. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, **Settings › Delete save data** also deletes the backup in your Play Games saved games. A backup made earlier stays in your Google account after an uninstall; you can delete it, together with your scores, in the Google Play Games app (Settings › Delete Play Games account and data) or at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com). Advertising and Play Games data are kept by Google under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID at any time as described above, and manage your Google data at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).

## Children

Looplight is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and want related advertising data removed, contact us and we will help.

## Security

Information sent between the game and Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
