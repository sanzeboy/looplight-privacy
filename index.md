# Looplight – Privacy Policy

*Effective 25 November 2026*

Looplight is a single-player arcade game for Android made by Sanjeeb Boyaju ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to **sanjeebboyaju@gmail.com**.

## What the game stores about you

**Your game progress.** Your coins and gems, your heroes, gear and talents, your best stages, your quests, daily rewards and calendar, the items you have bought, and your settings (sound and vibration). This is saved in a file on your device only. It is not uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time in **Settings › Delete save data**.

**No account with us.** Looplight has no account of its own. We do not ask for your name, your email address or any other contact details.

We do not collect your contacts, your photos, your files, your microphone or camera, or your precise location. The game has no chat and no online multiplayer.

## In-app purchases

You can buy gems, a starter pack and "Remove ads" in the game's shop. Purchases are handled by Google Play: you pay Google, and Google tells the game which item you bought so that it can be given to you. We do not receive your payment details. Your purchase history is kept by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/). The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to Google Play.

## Advertising

Looplight is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob: occasional full-screen ads between runs (never during play, and none at all after you buy "Remove ads"), and optional video ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game reward, such as a revive, double coins, a reroll or a Flare start. There are no banner ads.

To show ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), information about your device, and information about how you interact with the ads, as well as crash and performance information about the ads SDK. Google uses this to show ads, to measure them, and to prevent fraud and abuse. This information goes to Google, not to us.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before AdMob uses your information for personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in **Settings › Privacy options**. Wherever you are, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Service providers

- **Google:** AdMob advertising, Google Play billing for in-app purchases, and Google Play for distributing the app. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Unity Technologies:** the game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity In-App Purchasing. The engine itself may collect basic, non-identifying crash and device information. See the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

We do not sell your information. We do not receive or store any personal information ourselves.

## How long information is kept, and deleting it

Your game progress stays on your device until you delete it in Settings, clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Each of these deletes it for good (items bought with real money can be restored from Google Play with **Settings › Restore purchases**). Advertising and purchase data are kept by Google under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID at any time as described above, and manage your Google data at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).

## Children

Looplight is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and want related advertising data removed, contact us and we will help.

## Security

Information sent between the game and Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
